
The Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard is an interactive Microsoft Excel data visualization project designed to analyze emergency room patient visits, waiting times, patient satisfaction, admission status, demographics, and department referrals.

The dashboard provides a consolidated monthly view of emergency room operations, helping users understand patient flow, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), and identify patterns in hospital service utilization.

The report includes interactive filters for year and month, allowing users to explore emergency room performance across different reporting periods.

Project Objectives
Monitor the total number of emergency room patients.
Analyze average patient waiting time.
Evaluate patient satisfaction scores.
Compare admitted and non-admitted patients.
Examine patient attendance status.
Understand the distribution of patients by gender and age group.
Identify the most frequently referred departments.
Support data-driven decision-making in hospital operations.

 Key Insights

Based on the values visible in the dashboard:

Patient volume: The report displays 479 patients.
Admission distribution: Approximately 50.52% of patients were not admitted, while 49.48% were admitted.
Age demographics: Patients aged 60–69 form the largest displayed age group.
Gender distribution: The male and female proportions are nearly equal.
Department referrals: General Practice leads among the named departments, while the None category accounts for the largest overall count.
Attendance status: The dashboard reports 57% under Delay and 43% under Ontime.
Operational monitoring: Waiting time and satisfaction score provide additional indicators for reviewing the patient experience.

These observations describe the displayed report and should not be interpreted as conclusions about overall hospital performance without reviewing the underlying dataset, definitions, and reporting period.
